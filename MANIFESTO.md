# Manifesto

*v0.1 draft — to be ratified by founding members.*

## Why we exist

AI has changed how software is made. Companies discovered they could ship with fewer people, and many of us — engineers with years of hard-won experience — were let go.

We do not believe our skills became worthless. We believe they became **unowned**. For decades our work created enormous value that flowed to others. The same tools that displaced us now make it possible for a small, coordinated group of experienced engineers to build what once took an entire company.

So we will build it ourselves — and own it together.

## Our mission

To unite displaced software engineers into a worker-owned enterprise that builds better software, web and cloud products than what exists today, and shares the value it creates fairly among the people who create it.

## What we believe

1. **AI-amplified, not anti-AI.** We don't fight the tools; we master them. One experienced engineer with AI is a team. A union of them is a company.
2. **Those who build should own.** The Union's products, revenue and decisions belong to its members — not to outside shareholders.
3. **Ideas can come from anyone.** Any member may propose what we build next. The community decides what we commit to.
4. **Contribution earns influence — but never control.** Everyone has a voice. Those who deliver earn more weight, within limits that keep power distributed.
5. **Small teams, real ownership.** The community decides *what* to build; teams decide *how*. We don't vote on every pull request.
6. **Craft matters.** We build software we'd be proud to use: reliable, respectful of users, honest in how it makes money.
7. **Radical transparency.** Decisions, finances and reputation are visible to all members.
8. **No one left behind.** We help members re-skill, find their place, and earn — whether through products, client work, or mentoring others.

## What we are not

- Not a charity. We intend to earn real income for real work.
- Not a protest movement. We are builders.
- Not a traditional startup. There are no founders who own it all and no exit to sell members out.

## Our promise

If you bring your skill and effort, the Union will give you a voice, a team, and a fair share of what we build together.
