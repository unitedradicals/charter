# IP & licensing

*v0.1 draft. Not legal advice — must be reviewed by a lawyer before the first member contributes production code.*

## Ownership
- **The Union owns the IP** of everything built under an approved proposal. No individual can withhold or relicense it.
- Members keep ownership of **pre-existing** work they bring, and grant the Union a perpetual license to use it in Union products (declared up front).
- Client work IP follows the client contract (normally assigned to the client); the Union retains reusable internal tooling where the contract allows.

## Default license
- **Proprietary / source-available by default.** Product code is private to members unless a project decides otherwise.
- A project may choose to open-source all or part of its code (e.g., SDKs, libraries) with a Standard vote, when that helps adoption or revenue.

## Contributor Agreement (outline)
Every member signs before contributing:
1. Assigns copyright of contributions to the Union's legal entity (or grants an exclusive license, depending on jurisdiction).
2. Confirms they have the right to contribute the work (no code owned by a former employer, no license violations).
3. In return, the Union guarantees the contributor's revenue rights per the [economic model](model.md).
4. Covers AI-generated code: members are responsible for reviewing it and must not knowingly include content that infringes third-party rights.

## Until a legal entity exists
Contributions are held in trust under the interim Contributor Agreement, to be transferred to the entity at formation. This is a real risk — forming the entity early is a priority (see [Roadmap](../roadmap.md)).
