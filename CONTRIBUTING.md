# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to build with us. During Phase 0, everything happens in this repository and in our community channels.

## Ways to contribute right now
1. **Improve the founding documents.** Open a pull request or discussion on any doc. Everything is v0.1 — challenge it.
2. **Answer an [open question](docs/open-questions.md).** Bring research, experience, or a concrete recommendation.
3. **Submit a proposal** for something the Union should build (see below).
4. **Back a proposal** you would personally commit hours to.
5. **Recruit.** Know a great engineer who was laid off? Invite them.

## Submitting a proposal
1. Open a new issue using the **Proposal** template (`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/proposal.md`).
2. Fill in every section — especially the **revenue path** and **effort estimate**.
3. Share it with the community and gather **5 committed backers**.
4. Follow the [Proposal lifecycle](docs/governance/proposal-lifecycle.md) from there.

## Backing a proposal
Comment on the proposal with:
```
I back this. Commitment: <hours/week>. Skills: <what you bring>.
```
Backing is a promise to work on it if it's approved — don't back casually.

## Contribution standards
- Follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Use AI tools freely — and review everything they produce. You are responsible for what you submit.
- Don't contribute code or material you don't have the right to share (e.g., a former employer's IP).
- Before contributing production code, sign the Contributor Agreement (see [IP & licensing](docs/economics/ip-and-licensing.md)).
