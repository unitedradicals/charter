# Charter

*v0.1 draft — provisional rules for the founding phase. Numbers marked **[placeholder]** will be confirmed by the first ratification vote.*

## 1. Purpose

The Union is a worker-owned collective that proposes, builds, and sells software products and services, and distributes the resulting value to its members according to contribution. See the [Manifesto](MANIFESTO.md).

## 2. Membership

- **2.1 Eligibility.** Open to software professionals (engineers, designers, PMs, QA, DevOps, etc.), with priority on those displaced by AI-driven layoffs.
- **2.2 Joining.** A candidate introduces themselves, agrees to this Charter and the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), and signs the Contributor Agreement (see [IP & licensing](docs/economics/ip-and-licensing.md)).
- **2.3 Member states.**
  - **Active** — has contributed in the last **90 days [placeholder]**. Counts toward quorum.
  - **Dormant** — keeps membership and earned revenue rights; does not count toward quorum. Becomes Active again on any contribution.
- **2.4 Leaving.** Members may leave at any time. Revenue shares already earned remain theirs per the [economic model](docs/economics/model.md).
- **2.5 Removal.** For serious Code of Conduct violations, by a ⅔ vote of the Stewards Council, appealable to a full member vote.

## 3. Roles

| Role | Responsibility | How chosen |
|---|---|---|
| **Member** | Propose, back, vote, contribute | Joins per §2 |
| **Project Lead** | Owns delivery of one approved project; forms the team | Elected by that project's backers |
| **Maintainer** | Owns code quality and technical decisions within a project | Appointed by Project Lead |
| **Stewards Council** | Runs the voting process, treasury, moderation, legal; does **not** decide what gets built | Elected by all members, **5 seats [placeholder]**, 6-month terms, max 2 consecutive |

During Phase 0, the founder(s) act as an interim Stewards Council until the first election.

## 4. Decision-making

- **4.1 What the community decides:** which proposals are funded/built, Charter amendments, Steward elections, revenue distribution rules, client contracts above a size threshold **[placeholder]**.
- **4.2 What teams decide:** architecture, tooling, task assignment, release timing — anything within an approved proposal's scope.
- **4.3 How votes work:** see [Voting](docs/governance/voting.md). Summary:
  - Every member has 1 base vote, plus contribution weight up to a **2× cap**.
  - Standard decisions: simple majority of weighted votes, with quorum.
  - Charter amendments and removal of Stewards: ⅔ majority.
- **4.4 Proposals:** follow the [Proposal lifecycle](docs/governance/proposal-lifecycle.md).

## 5. Transparency

All votes, proposals, treasury balances, revenue distributions, and reputation scores are visible to all members.

## 6. Treasury

Revenue is collected by the Union's legal entity and distributed per the [economic model](docs/economics/model.md). Stewards publish a treasury report every **month [placeholder]**.

## 7. Amendments

Any member may propose an amendment using the standard proposal process. Amendments require a ⅔ weighted majority and a quorum of **30% of Active members [placeholder]**.

## 8. Ratification

This Charter takes effect when approved by a simple majority of the first **25 founding members [placeholder]**. Until then, it serves as a working draft.
