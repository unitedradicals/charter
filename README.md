## What is this?

AI has reshaped the software industry, and many skilled engineers lost their jobs in the process. **United Radicals** (also called "the Union" in these docs) brings them together into a **worker-owned enterprise** where members:

- **Propose** ideas for products and services worth building
- **Vote** on which ideas the Union commits to
- **Build** them in small, empowered teams — amplified by AI, not replaced by it
- **Share** the revenue they generate, in proportion to contribution

## How it works (short version)

1. Anyone in the Union submits a **proposal**.
2. Once it gathers **5 committed backers**, it goes to a **community vote**.
3. Approved proposals get a **team**, a **lead**, and a **plan**.
4. The team builds and ships. Revenue flows to the Union treasury and to contributors.

Details: [Proposal lifecycle](docs/governance/proposal-lifecycle.md) · [Voting](docs/governance/voting.md)

## How we earn

| Stream | Role |
|---|---|
| **Co-op products** | Core. Products the Union builds, owns and sells. |
| **Client work** | Secondary. The Union takes contracts and staffs them with members. |
| **Sponsorships** | Supplementary. Patrons fund the Union for early access and roadmap input. |
