# Economic model

*v0.1 draft — principles only. Exact formulas depend on the legal entity chosen (see [Legal entity options](legal-entity-options.md)).*

Members need income, not just purpose. The Union has three revenue streams, in priority order.

## 1. Co-op products — the core
Products proposed, voted on, and built by members (see [Proposal lifecycle](../governance/proposal-lifecycle.md)). Owned collectively by the Union and sold as SaaS, licenses, subscriptions, etc.

**Distribution of product revenue [placeholder percentages]:**

| Share | Recipient | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 60% | **Project contributors** | Split by contribution to *that* product (hours + reputation ledger) |
| 25% | **Union treasury** | Infrastructure, legal, new-project seed costs, reserves |
| 15% | **All Active members** | Solidarity dividend — rewards the shared commons (reviews, governance, community) |

## 2. Client work — secondary
The Union operates as a collective agency, taking paid contracts and staffing them with members.

- The member doing the work receives the large majority of the fee — **85% [placeholder]**.
- The remainder goes to the treasury (sales, contracts, insurance, admin).
- Contracts above **€25k [placeholder]** require a Standard vote, mainly to check capacity and reputational fit.
- Client work bridges income for members while products mature — it's how the Union pays people *early*.

## 3. Sponsorships — supplementary
Patrons (companies or individuals) who believe in the mission fund the Union. Our code is **not required to be open source**, so sponsorship is framed as patronage with tangible benefits:

| Tier | Example benefits |
|---|---|
| Supporter | Name on site, quarterly update |
| Partner | Early/beta access to Union products, discounts |
| Patron | Roadmap advisory input (non-voting), priority for client work, co-branding |

Sponsorship money goes **100% to the treasury** and is allocated by the Stewards under a published budget. Sponsors never get votes.

> ⚠️ Most sponsorship platforms (GitHub Sponsors, Open Collective) are designed around open-source work. Closed-source sponsorship will likely rely on direct agreements. See [Open questions](../open-questions.md).

## Principles
1. **Earned stays earned.** Leaving the Union doesn't forfeit revenue already allocated to you.
2. **Treasury first for survival, members first for surplus.** The treasury holds a minimum reserve — **6 months of fixed costs [placeholder]** — before discretionary spending.
3. **Monthly, transparent books.** Every member can see where every euro/dollar came from and went.
4. **No outside equity that buys control.** Investment, if ever accepted, must not carry governance rights over members.
