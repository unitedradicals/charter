# Code of Conduct

*Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.*

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in the Union a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Many of us arrive after losing a job. We treat each other with patience and dignity.

## Our standards
**Expected:**
- Empathy and kindness toward other members
- Respect for differing opinions, experiences, and votes
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the Union as a whole

**Unacceptable:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment, or publishing others' private information without permission
- **Vote manipulation:** duplicate accounts, vote buying, coordinated brigading
- **Reputation gaming:** inflating contributions or awarding points dishonestly
- Contributing material you don't have the rights to
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement
The Stewards Council (or the interim founders during Phase 0) is responsible for enforcement. Reports are handled confidentially. *(Reporting contact to be announced.)*

| Level | Example | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Correction | Minor inappropriate behavior | Private warning |
| 2. Warning | Repeated or more serious violation | Formal warning; temporary loss of voting rights possible |
| 3. Temporary suspension | Serious or sustained violation | Suspension from participation for a set period |
| 4. Removal | Pattern of violations, harassment, fraud | Removal per [Charter §2.5](CHARTER.md), appealable to a member vote |
